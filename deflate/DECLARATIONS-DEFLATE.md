## The oracle (X5 (a)), to be pinned BEFORE its first run
zlib's `inflate` in RAW mode (windowBits −15), built in scratch from the official release tarball. The release is named by version
and its sha256 is read at download and recorded beside the first run. Python's bundled zlib (1.2.12) is the CORPUS compressor
only, and is not the oracle.

**The protocol:**
- A stream is ACCEPTED iff `inflate` returns `Z_STREAM_END`.
- `Z_BUF_ERROR` (truncated input) and `Z_DATA_ERROR` are REJECTED.
- Bytes after the final block are ignored, as in Appendix S's S2.
- REASONS are compared and reported (D1 predicts ≥ 1 reason disagreement), never scored.
