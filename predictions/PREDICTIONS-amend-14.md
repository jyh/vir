## The ruling (vir, predictions owner)
1. **W1 is scored on the VIR L1 theories alone**, the `virl1_*` files on MetaPRL and `VIRL1/*` on Lean. W1 asks whether
   an L1 CONSTRUCT is primitive, and a prim in `itt_bar` stands for PARTIALITY, not for an L1 construct. This is W1's own
   text ("no `prim` in a VIR L1 theory"), applied to the file the prim lives in. That is the narrowing amend-12 forbade only in
   the other direction: amend-12 stops cost leaving the VIR-side column by a file move, and this ruling moves nothing out of
   VIR-side.
2. **`itt_bar` is VIR-side, in its own column, PARTIALITY THEORY (built by the arm, not at the pin): 24 prims / 4 derived as
   the arm reports them, re-counted by the harness from the file.** That column is printed BESIDE W1 and inside W1b's MetaPRL
   figure, with the label NOT-AT-PIN. So W1b reads: MetaPRL framework at the pin (reflection theories, integers) +
   PARTIALITY THEORY (NOT-AT-PIN), against Lean's axioms (mathlib's `Part` is at the pin and adds no axiom). W1b's prediction
   (MetaPRL > Lean) is scored on the pin figure ALONE, and the sum is printed beside it. The direction was predicted for the
   logics as released, and the partiality theory is reported, never hidden.
3. **No re-pin (arm (b) is declined).** Landing `itt_bar` in metaprl and moving the pin after this amendment would make
   VIR-campaign work framework by a merge. That is exactly the move amend-12 rules out. If `itt_bar` lands in metaprl
   later, it is reported as a FUTURE framework addition, and the L1 counts stay at fb8e715d7.
4. **The Lean side, symmetrically:** mathlib's `Part`, `Part.fix` and `Mathlib.Control.LawfulFix` are at the pin and built,
   so they are FRAMEWORK. Lean's partiality column is 0 lemmas written. Its continuity proof (`Φ_cont`) is VIR-side and is
   W6b's count.
