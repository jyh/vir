# Errata to OBJECT-L5 (O95, layer L5)

`../object-L5/OBJECT-L5.md` is never edited after its pin (vir a35589c). Each defect gets a row here, with who found it and what
each arm does about it. Both arms get the same decision.

| id | where | the defect | found by | decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L5-E1 | OBJECT-L5 §1 J1 (a) (*"a function type may carry the capabilities its body uses, a fix body is checked with those capabilities in Δ"*) and J3 (a) (copy, compare: two regions each) | **J1 (a) says how capabilities get INTO a body and is silent on how they get OUT, and under L2's rules there is no way out for two.** OBJECT-L2 §4: `ret a` with τ ≠ cap needs Δ = ∅, and `ret c` returns ONE capability (OBJECT-L3 R-a); a product cannot carry one. And `store` CONSUMES its capability and binds a new one (`let c′:cap ← store(c, a₁, a₂) in e`), so a body that writes its region does not end holding the variable it was given. So a routine body that writes or merely keeps two regions (copy src → dst, compare a b) has no typed ending: it cannot return both capabilities, and it may not drop them (linear, not affine). J3 (a)'s copy is untypable as written, and so is compare. | vir (the Lean arm), 2026-11-13, reading ⊢₂'s λ/fix and `ret` rules (`l2/VIRL2/Typing2.lean`, `HasTypeA₂.lam`/`.fix`, `HasType₂.app`) to write its L5 declarations, after the pin and amend-24, before any L5 file on either arm | **A capability-carrying function LENDS its capabilities and the call binds them back, one variable each.** (1) The type is `τ₁ →[k] τ₂`: an argument of type τ₁ (cap-free, as at L2) and k ≥ 0 capabilities; `τ₁ →[0] τ₂` is L2's `τ₁ → τ₂`. (2) A λ/fix body is checked with Δ = the k lent capabilities (fix: plus nothing for the self variable, which is cap-free). (3) Its ending is `ret a [d₁ … d_k]`: the cap-free result a, with Δ = {d₁ … d_k} EXACTLY, so every lent capability goes back, in order, and none is freed or leaked. (4) The call is an effect binder, as `store` is: `let x:τ₂, c₁′ … c_k′ ⇐ f a [c₁ … c_k] in e`, where the cᵢ are DISTINCT members of the caller's Δ, removed, and the cᵢ′ are bound in their place. That is `store`'s own shape (consume c, bind c′), k times, and L4-E1's shape (single-capability binders, never a tuple type). (5) That cᵢ′ names the SAME region as cᵢ, with the same size, is a fact of the LOGIC (each routine's triple), never of a type (OBJECT-L2's bounds-by-logic, carried up). **This is the narrowest form of OBJECT-L3 G2(b)'s trigger again** (k single-capability binders, no capability inside a product) and it adds no type former beyond the annotated arrow J1 (a) already names. amend-24 is unchanged: LV1–LV11 are scored on this form, and LV3's CE reads k = 0 as L2's arrow. |
